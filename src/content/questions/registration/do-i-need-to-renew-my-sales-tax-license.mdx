Most states: no renewal needed. Your permit stays active as long as you keep filing. Florida is the important exception for ecommerce sellers, its Annual Resale Certificate must be renewed each year or it expires December 31.

## Key takeaways

- **Most states:** sales tax permits are issued without an expiration date and do not require renewal, they remain valid indefinitely as long as returns are filed
- **Florida:** the Annual Resale Certificate (DR-13) expires every December 31 and must be renewed: this is a common compliance gap for ecommerce sellers with Florida nexus
- **A few states** issue permits with fixed terms (typically 1–2 years) that require renewal notices, watch for renewal notices from your state DOR and keep contact information current so they reach you
- If you stop filing returns, some states will administratively revoke your permit after a period of inactivity: this is different from a renewal requirement
- SST states registered through a CSP: the CSP manages permit status; confirm with your CSP whether any renewal actions are required
- Keep your registered email address and mailing address current with each state's DOR, renewal notices sent to a stale address are your responsibility to catch
