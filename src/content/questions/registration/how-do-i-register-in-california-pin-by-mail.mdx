The PIN isn't needed to register or collect, it's needed to grant third-party access to your account. Register online at CDTFA.ca.gov, begin collecting immediately, and wait for the PIN to arrive before connecting your compliance provider to the account.

## Key takeaways

- **Registration itself:** complete online at CDTFA.ca.gov; takes 20–30 minutes; permit is issued upon completion
- **PIN-by-mail:** California mails a security PIN to your business address to authorize third-party access (your accountant, TaxCloud, or other compliance software); allow 1–3 weeks for delivery
- You can collect California sales tax from the registration date without the PIN, collection authority doesn't require it
- The PIN is required before your compliance software or accountant can access your CDTFA account, file returns on your behalf, or make payments
- Use your actual business mailing address during registration: the PIN goes to the address on file, and a stale or PO Box address will delay the process
- Once you receive the PIN, activate third-party access within your CDTFA account under "Account Access" and share the credentials with your provider
- If the PIN doesn't arrive after 3 weeks, contact CDTFA at 1-800-400-7115 to request a replacement
