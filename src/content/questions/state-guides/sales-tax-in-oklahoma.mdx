Oklahoma has a relatively low state rate of 4.5% but significant county and city additions that push combined rates to 8%–11% in major cities. Oklahoma is not an SST member state and must be registered directly with the Oklahoma Tax Commission. A seller-favorable rule: separately stated shipping is not taxable. Oklahoma also has a complex food taxability structure, groceries are subject to Oklahoma sales tax with no general exemption at the state level.

## Economic nexus

Oklahoma's OR threshold: $100,000 in gross sales OR 200 or more separate transactions into Oklahoma in the current or prior calendar year. Either condition alone triggers registration.

Oklahoma enacted its economic nexus rules effective November 1, 2019.

## Physical nexus

Physical presence in Oklahoma creates nexus without any threshold:
- Warehouse, office, or storage facility in Oklahoma
- Amazon FBA inventory in Oklahoma fulfillment centers
- Employees, agents, or independent contractors in Oklahoma
- Sales representatives in Oklahoma

## Registration

Register through the Oklahoma Tax Commission's OkTAP portal (oktap.tax.ok.gov). Registration is free. Oklahoma issues a Sales Tax Permit.

## Tax rates

**State rate:** 4.5%

**County and city additions:** Oklahoma's local rate structure involves hundreds of jurisdictions. County rates and city rates each layer on the state rate. Both can apply simultaneously for addresses within incorporated municipalities.

**Combined rate examples:**
- Oklahoma City: 8.625% combined (4.5% state + county + city)
- Tulsa: 8.517% combined
- Norman: 9.375% combined
- Broken Arrow: 8.417% combined
- Some smaller municipalities with additional special taxes: 10%–11%+ combined

Oklahoma uses destination sourcing: the rate applied is based on the delivery address. Address-level rate lookup is necessary for accurate Oklahoma calculations.

## What's taxable

**Generally taxable:** Electronics, clothing, furniture, sporting goods, toys, food (see below), most tangible personal property.

**Generally exempt:**
- Prescription medications
- Agricultural equipment and supplies
- Manufacturing machinery

**Notable Oklahoma rules:**
- **Food:** Oklahoma taxes food and food ingredients at the full state rate, there is no general grocery exemption. Groceries are subject to 4.5% state tax plus applicable local rates in Oklahoma. This is one of the more notable differences from neighboring states
- **Clothing:** Taxable in Oklahoma, no clothing exemption
- **Digital products:** Oklahoma taxes specified digital products including downloaded software and digital audio/audiovisual works
- **SaaS:** Oklahoma has taken the position that remotely accessed software (SaaS) is taxable in certain circumstances

## Shipping taxability

Oklahoma does not tax separately stated delivery charges. When shipping is listed as a separate line item on the invoice, it is not subject to Oklahoma sales tax.

When shipping is bundled with the sale price, it may be taxable if the goods are taxable.

**Practical implication:** Separately state delivery charges on all Oklahoma invoices.

## Marketplace facilitator rules

Oklahoma enacted marketplace facilitator legislation effective July 1, 2018, among the earliest of any state. Qualifying marketplace facilitators collect and remit Oklahoma sales tax on marketplace-facilitated sales.

Remote sellers with no Oklahoma physical nexus whose only Oklahoma sales are through marketplace facilitators may not need to separately register. Sellers with Oklahoma physical nexus must register regardless.

## State-specific notes

**Food is taxable:** Oklahoma does not exempt groceries. Sellers of food products (including pantry staples, snack foods, and specialty foods) should collect Oklahoma sales tax at the full rate on food deliveries to Oklahoma addresses.

**Shipping exemption:** Oklahoma's separately stated shipping exemption is a meaningful operational consideration. Configure your checkout to display shipping separately on Oklahoma orders.

**Rate complexity despite low state rate:** Oklahoma's 4.5% state rate appears low, but local additions push effective rates to 8%–11% in most populated areas. Rural addresses with no county or city additions remain at 4.5%, but these represent a small share of orders for most ecommerce sellers.
