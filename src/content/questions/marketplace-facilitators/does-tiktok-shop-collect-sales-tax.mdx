Yes. TikTok Shop operates as a marketplace facilitator in the United States and collects and remits sales tax on behalf of sellers for transactions in all states with a general sales tax.

Sellers on TikTok Shop don't configure tax collection for those transactions — TikTok Shop handles it at checkout and remits to each state. This follows the same model as Amazon, Etsy, Walmart Marketplace, and eBay.

## TikTok Shop as an emerging channel

TikTok Shop launched in the US market in 2023 and grew rapidly through 2024. As a marketplace facilitator from its US launch, it came into compliance with state marketplace facilitator laws directly, sellers didn't need to reconfigure their approach as they would have in 2018–2019 when the marketplace facilitator laws were first enacted.

For brands selling through TikTok Shop, the sales tax mechanics are the same as any other covered marketplace.

## The same caveats apply

TikTok Shop's collection covers TikTok Shop transactions. It doesn't cover:

- Your Shopify or own-website sales
- Sales through Amazon, Etsy, or other channels
- Registration obligations created by physical nexus
- Your economic nexus threshold — TikTok Shop sales count in most states

For sellers using TikTok Shop as one channel among several, this means TikTok Shop adds to your total threshold accumulation across all channels, and to the combined picture of where you have nexus obligations for direct-channel sales.

## Verify current status

TikTok Shop's US operations have been subject to regulatory and legislative attention. Regardless of the platform's status at any given point, sales made through TikTok Shop while it was operating are covered by marketplace facilitator collection for that period. Sellers' obligations on other channels are unaffected by TikTok Shop's regulatory status.
