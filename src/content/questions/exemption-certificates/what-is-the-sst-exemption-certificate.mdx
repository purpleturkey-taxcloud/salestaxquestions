The SST Exemption Certificate is a single standardized form accepted in all 24 SST member states. One certificate, one buyer, works across all SST states.

## Key takeaways

- **Accepted in all 24 SST member states:** TX, WA, GA, NC, OH, IN, WI, MN, NE, SD, IA, KS, KY, NV, NJ, RI, OK, AR, UT, VT, WY, WV, ID, and MI (note: specific membership list can evolve; confirm current members at streamlinedsalestax.org)
- **Single form, multiple states:** the buyer fills out one SST certificate indicating exemption reason; the seller accepts it for all SST-state transactions with that buyer
- **Accepted by CSP-enrolled sellers:** sellers who file through a Certified Service Provider (CSP) like TaxCloud are automatically required to accept the SST certificate: the CSP program standards mandate it
- **Exemption reasons covered:** the SST certificate covers all standard exemption types: resale, nonprofit, government, manufacturing, agriculture, and others: the buyer checks the applicable reason
- **Good-faith protection:** sellers who accept SST certificates in good faith receive audit liability protection in SST member states; if the buyer provided fraudulent information, the seller is shielded
- **Not valid in non-SST states:** the SST certificate is not accepted in CA, TX (non-SST), FL, NY, AZ, CO, IL, and other non-SST states, state-specific or MTC certificates are needed for those states
