At $20M GMV, the sales tax stack question is not "do I need enterprise software?" — it's "which platform delivers the functionality I need without enterprise pricing?" The compliance requirements at this scale are well-defined; the price variation across platforms that meet those requirements is enormous.

## What $20M GMV actually requires from a compliance stack

**Calculation:** Rooftop-level rate accuracy for every ship-to jurisdiction. The calculation engine must handle city, county, and special district rates, not just state-level or zip-code rates. This is a baseline requirement, not a premium feature.

**Multi-state filing:** AutoFile across all nexus states, covering both SST and non-SST states. Filing must handle multiple frequencies (monthly, quarterly, annual) per state simultaneously, and must file zero returns automatically when no liability is due.

**SST enrollment:** For the SST-state subset of your nexus footprint, enrollment through a Certified Service Provider covers calculation and filing at no charge. This is not a premium add-on, it's the baseline for any platform that functions as a CSP.

**Channel integration:** Transaction data from all selling channels must flow into the calculation and filing platform accurately — Shopify, Amazon, Etsy, wholesale, and any other active channel.

**Exemption certificate management:** Required if you have B2B volume. The certificate program needs collection routing, validity verification, expiration tracking, and audit-ready storage. Can be native to the platform or through an integrated third-party tool.

**Notice management:** The platform should surface or route state notices, at minimum, provide a clear record of filings submitted so you can respond to state inquiries with confidence.

## The platform landscape at mid-market scale

### TaxCloud

TaxCloud is a Certified Service Provider in the SST program, which means SST-state calculation and filing are included at no charge. For a 35-state nexus footprint with 22 SST states, 22 states are covered under the SST program. The remaining 13 non-SST states are handled through the AutoFile platform.

Annual cost for a $20M brand: $4,000–$8,000 depending on transaction volume and non-SST state count. Integrations with Shopify, WooCommerce, Magento, and custom platforms via API.

**Best for:** Brands with significant SST-state nexus where the CSP structure provides both cost savings and audit liability benefit.

### TaxJar

Transaction-based pricing with AutoFile for all states. Not a CSP, so SST states are billed the same as non-SST states. Annual cost for a $20M brand: $8,000–$18,000 depending on transaction volume.

Shopify, BigCommerce, Amazon, and other marketplace integrations. Clean UI and reporting interface.

**Best for:** Brands with simpler nexus footprints concentrated in non-SST states, or brands where the Shopify integration depth is the primary concern.

### Avalara AvaTax + Returns

Enterprise platform with per-transaction calculation pricing plus per-return filing fees. Annual cost for a $20M brand typically runs $25,000–$50,000 with volume overages.

Deep integration library (400+ connectors), enterprise support model, bundled CertCapture for exemption certificates.

**Best for:** Brands with complex integration requirements across multiple ERPs and systems, or brands where the parent company has enterprise Avalara licensing. Not cost-competitive for most standalone mid-market brands.

### Vertex

Enterprise platform similar to Avalara in pricing and complexity. Designed for large enterprises with complex tax needs. Annual cost for a $20M brand would run $30,000–$60,000+.

**Best for:** Enterprise and near-enterprise brands with SAP, Oracle, or complex multi-ERP environments. Not right for most $20M eCommerce brands.

## The recommended stack at $20M GMV

For most $20M GMV eCommerce brands with 20–35 states of nexus:

| Component | Solution | Annual cost |
|---|---|---|
| Calculation + AutoFile + SST | TaxCloud (CSP) | $4,000–$8,000 |
| Exemption certificates (if B2B) | Native or integrated tool | $2,000–$5,000 |
| Amazon transaction data | Amazon Seller Central API integration | Included |
| SALT advisor (retainer) | Fractional SALT CPA | $5,000–$10,000 |
| **Total** | | **$11,000–$23,000/year** |

The same brand on Avalara would spend $35,000–$60,000+ for equivalent filing coverage, before the SALT advisor relationship.

## When to switch platforms

If your current stack has any of these problems, it's worth evaluating alternatives:

- **Rate accuracy issues**: zip-code-level rates generating under-collection complaints from states
- **Pricing escalation**: annual true-up provisions pushing cost significantly above the original contract
- **SST states not covered under CSP program**: paying per-state filing for states that should be SST-covered
- **Manual data handling**: no automated integration with your primary commerce platform, requiring CSV imports every period
- **Missing states**: the platform doesn't cover all the states in your nexus footprint

The switching cost for a compliance platform is real but manageable, typically 40–80 hours of configuration and testing spread over a 60–90 day transition period.
