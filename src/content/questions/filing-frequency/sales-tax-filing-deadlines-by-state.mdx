## Important caveats

- **These are general patterns.** Your assigned due date may differ based on your filing frequency, enrollment date, or state-specific rules. Always confirm your exact due date in your state DOR portal or welcome letter.
- **Filing frequency affects due dates.** Monthly filers and quarterly filers often have different due dates in the same state.
- **Electronic filing may extend due dates.** Several states (CA, FL, MA) give additional days to filers who pay electronically. Check your state's e-file program details.
- **Prepayment requirements:** CA requires prepayment by the 24th for sellers above $17,000 monthly liability; NY has prepayment requirements for large quarterly filers.
- **Home rule cities (CO):** Denver, Boulder, Aurora, and other Colorado home rule cities have their own due dates, separate from the state return.
- **Louisiana parishes:** Most Louisiana parishes administer their own returns with their own due dates, separate from the state return filed through LARC.
