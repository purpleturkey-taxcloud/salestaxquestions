Shopify Tax is a better calculator than Shopify's defaults. It's not a compliance platform, filing still requires a separate solution.

## Key takeaways

- **Better rate data:** Shopify Tax uses a third-party rate database with more frequent updates than Shopify's standard tax settings; reduces rate errors in complex jurisdictions
- **Product categorization:** Shopify Tax includes a product category mapping interface that helps sellers assign the correct taxability treatment to products; improves handling of state-variable categories like clothing, food, and digital goods
- **Nexus insights:** Shopify Tax tracks your sales by state and shows approaching nexus thresholds; a meaningful upgrade from manually monitoring sales data
- **What it still doesn't do:** file returns, remit tax to states, provide AutoFile, or give SST CSP certification (audit protection in SST states)
- **Pricing:** Shopify Tax is included on Shopify plans (not Basic); pricing tiers affect feature access; check current Shopify pricing
- **Who it's right for:** sellers who want improved accuracy within Shopify's native experience without managing a third-party integration; sellers who file manually or use an accountant for returns
- **Who should use a third-party engine instead:** sellers who want AutoFile, need SST CSP audit protection (TaxCloud), have complex product taxability, or have high monthly liability where calculation errors create material risk
