No — Shopify is a collection tool, not a compliance tool. It collects the tax; you file and remit it.

## Key takeaways

- **Shopify collects, you remit:** tax collected at checkout goes into your payment proceeds; Shopify does not make payments to state DORs
- **No returns filed by Shopify:** Shopify does not prepare or submit sales tax returns to any state; you (or your software/accountant) must do this
- **Not a custodian of tax funds:** collected sales tax is not held in a separate account; it's part of your gross revenue; you must set aside funds for remittance on your own
- **Where filing/remittance comes from:** use Shopify's Sales Tax Report to get the data → file returns yourself through each state's portal, or enroll in AutoFile through a connected tax engine (TaxCloud, Avalara, TaxJar)
- **TaxCloud integration:** when TaxCloud is connected to Shopify, it handles rate calculation and AutoFile, filing and remittance in enrolled states happens automatically; this is the combination that makes Shopify a complete compliance solution
- **Sellers discover this late:** one of the most common compliance surprises is a seller who has been collecting tax via Shopify for months, assuming it was being handled, only to discover they have 6 months of unremitted collected tax sitting in their bank account
- **Action item:** if you've been collecting via Shopify and not filing, check your Shopify Sales Tax Report, calculate how much you've collected by state, and address those open periods
