BigCommerce includes reasonable native tax calculation, and on higher plans bundles an Avalara AvaTax integration, making it one of the more capable platforms out of the box.

## Key takeaways

- **Native tax engine:** BigCommerce's built-in provider calculates rates at checkout using the customer's ship-to address; no plugin required for basic automated rate lookup
- **Avalara AvaTax bundled:** on higher-tier BigCommerce plans, Avalara AvaTax is included as an integrated provider; the integration is bundled but Avalara's per-transaction fees apply separately
- **Product tax classes:** BigCommerce supports product tax codes and tax class assignments; sellers can map products to different tax classes (e.g., clothing, digital goods, reduced-rate items) to apply state-specific taxability rules
- **Avalara vs. native engine:** Avalara provides deeper product taxability coverage and more frequently updated rates than the native BigCommerce engine; for sellers with complex product catalogs, the Avalara integration is the better choice
- **No filing or remittance:** BigCommerce collects tax at checkout; it does not file state returns or remit collected tax to state revenue agencies; filing is the seller's responsibility
- **TaxCloud integration:** TaxCloud can be connected to BigCommerce via API; third-party connector options exist; this is the path for sellers who want SST CSP certification and AutoFile in SST states
- **Manual rate tables:** BigCommerce also supports manual tax rules for sellers who prefer to manage rates themselves; not recommended for multi-state compliance due to maintenance burden
