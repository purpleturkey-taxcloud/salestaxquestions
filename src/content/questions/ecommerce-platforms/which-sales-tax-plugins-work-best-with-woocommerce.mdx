Three certified engines have established WooCommerce integrations. The right one depends on your volume, complexity, and whether SST certification matters.

## Key takeaways

**TaxJar for WooCommerce (now part of Stripe):**
- Native WooCommerce plugin with simple setup
- Automated rate lookup at checkout
- AutoFile available through TaxJar platform
- Best for small-to-mid-size sellers wanting easy, low-friction setup
- Pricing: transaction-based; check current rates

**Avalara AvaTax for WooCommerce:**
- WooCommerce plugin connecting to Avalara's AvaTax engine
- Largest product tax code library in the industry
- AutoFile (Avalara Returns) available separately
- Better for complex product catalogs and enterprise needs
- Higher price point; per-transaction pricing
- Best for large sellers or those with extensive product taxability complexity

**TaxCloud:**
- SST Certified Service Provider: the only CSP-certified option
- Free calculation and filing in SST member states (covered under the SST CSP contract)
- API integration with WooCommerce via connector or custom integration
- AutoFile available
- Best for sellers who want SST audit protection and lower cost in SST states

**Other considerations:**
- Verify the plugin's WordPress/WooCommerce version compatibility before installing
- Check that the plugin integrates with your other WooCommerce plugins (subscription plugins, B2B plugins, etc.) before committing
- All three options require an account with the respective platform; the plugin is a connector, not a standalone tool
