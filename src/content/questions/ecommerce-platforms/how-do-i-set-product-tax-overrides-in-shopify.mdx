Product overrides are the mechanism for handling state-specific exemptions in Shopify when you're not using a third-party engine.

## Key takeaways

- **Per-product toggle:** each product has a "Charge taxes on this product" checkbox in the product details; unchecking it makes the item tax-exempt in all states, use only for universally non-taxable items
- **State-specific overrides:** for products taxable in most states but exempt in some (clothing, food), use Settings > Taxes and duties > Tax overrides; create an override for the state + product collection combination
- **Collection-based overrides:** Shopify overrides work at the product collection level; put exempt products in a dedicated collection (e.g., "Exempt Clothing PA") and apply the override to that collection
- **Rate override vs. 0%:** overrides can set a specific rate (e.g., Illinois 1% for food) or a 0% exempt rate; use the appropriate rate for the state's treatment
- **Override limitations:** Shopify's native override system is manageable for a handful of states with simple exemptions; for sellers with complex product catalogs across many states, the override system becomes unwieldy: this is a key reason to connect a third-party tax engine that applies product tax codes automatically
- **Third-party engine replaces overrides:** when TaxCloud, Avalara, or TaxJar is connected, their product tax codes handle state-specific taxability automatically; Shopify overrides become redundant and should be removed to avoid conflicts
- **Testing overrides:** after setting an override, place a test order shipping to the exempt state and confirm $0 tax is applied; a 5-minute test prevents months of overcollection
