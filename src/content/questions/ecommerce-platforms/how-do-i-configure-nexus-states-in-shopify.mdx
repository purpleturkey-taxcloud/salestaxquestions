Nexus configuration in Shopify tells the platform where to calculate and collect tax. Only configure states where you're actually registered.

## Key takeaways

- **Location:** Settings > Taxes and duties > United States > Tax regions
- **Adding a state:** select the state from the list; Shopify begins calculating applicable state + local tax rates for orders shipping there
- **Only add registered states:** Shopify will collect tax as soon as a state is added; if you're not registered, you have no way to legally remit what you collect; don't add states until you're registered
- **Shopify uses your store address for home-state rates:** your primary store address determines which state's rules apply when you ship from your location; this affects nexus in your home state
- **Overrides within a state:** for states with product-specific exemptions, Shopify allows per-product or per-collection tax overrides; use these for categories with state-variable taxability (clothing, food, etc.)
- **Third-party engine replaces this:** if you connect a certified tax engine (TaxCloud, Avalara, TaxJar) via API, that engine handles all rate calculation; the Shopify nexus configuration becomes secondary or irrelevant depending on the integration
- **Removing a state:** if you close a permit (e.g., nexus drops below threshold and you de-register), remove the state from your Shopify configuration so you stop collecting tax you can no longer remit
- **Shopify doesn't alert on nexus threshold breach:** if your sales into a non-configured state cross the economic nexus threshold, Shopify won't tell you; nexus monitoring must be done separately
