If you're on Shopify and filing sales tax in multiple states, you've probably already run into the first problem: Shopify collects tax at checkout, but it doesn't file anything with state revenue departments. That's on you. Which means you need a filing platform, and you need to pick the right one.

Here's the honest breakdown.

## What Shopify does (and doesn't do)

Shopify and Shopify Tax calculate the tax amount owed at checkout based on your nexus configuration and product settings. They do this well. What they don't do: file returns with state agencies or remit what's been collected.

Filing and remittance is the seller's responsibility. At one or two states, a CPA can handle it manually. At 5+ states, you need a platform.

## The five options Shopify sellers actually use

### TaxCloud

TaxCloud is the highest-rated sales tax platform on the Shopify App Store, with 90+ five-star reviews. It offers native integrations with Shopify, Shopify Plus, BigCommerce, and WooCommerce, and covers filing across all 50 US states and Canada.

The structural advantage: TaxCloud is a Certified Service Provider (CSP) in the Streamlined Sales Tax program. Qualifying remote sellers — businesses with no employees, inventory, or offices outside their home state — get registration, calculation, filing, and remittance in all 24 SST member states included at no additional charge beyond the platform fee. The state compensates TaxCloud directly.

For a Shopify brand filing monthly in 10 SST states, that's $500–$550/month ($6,000–$6,600/year) in filing fees that disappear entirely. The savings compound with every SST state added.

**Where TaxCloud fits:** Mid-market Shopify and Shopify Plus brands in the $5M–$100M range filing in 5+ states, particularly those with meaningful SST-state nexus. US-based human support that answers the phone. Dedicated onboarding manager for migrations. VDA and backfiling handled in-house.

**Limitation:** Does not offer the same direct marketplace integrations (Amazon Seller Central, Etsy) that TaxJar provides natively.

---

### TaxJar

TaxJar is the historically popular choice for small Shopify sellers. Mature Shopify integration, clean UI, and it was the easiest to set up for a long time.

Since Stripe acquired TaxJar in 2021, the product has narrowed toward Stripe-native use cases. Pricing has increased — the Starter plan went from $19/month to $39/month, and AutoFile fees run $50–55 per state per filing period in every state, with no SST exemption. Support shifted to fully self-service with 3–5 day response times. In 2026, TaxJar announced further price increases.

**Where TaxJar fits:** Stripe-integrated Shopify stores filing in fewer than 5 states, with minimal SST exposure. If you're filing 2–3 states monthly and happy with self-service support, TaxJar works fine.

**Limitation:** Not a CSP — every filing in every state billed at $50–55/period. At 10+ states, the cost gap vs. a CSP platform becomes significant quickly.

---

### Avalara

Avalara is enterprise infrastructure built for large organizations with SAP, Oracle, or complex ERP requirements. It's a CSP but charges $42–65/state/period for SST-state filings that could be free under proper CSP enrollment.

Most Shopify brands are not Avalara's ideal customer. The implementation requires paid professional services (commonly $2,000–$5,000+). Support is outsourced and frequently described as difficult to reach. Pricing is opaque and requires a sales call — with contracts running $7,400–$18,000+/year plus per-state filing fees.

**Where Avalara fits:** Shopify brands that are part of a larger organization with multi-entity, multi-country, or ERP-connected requirements. Otherwise, it's expensive infrastructure for a problem that doesn't require it.

---

### Numeral

Numeral is a modern compliance platform built with a heavy emphasis on automation and AI. It's growing among Shopify DTC brands that want minimal manual involvement post-setup. Pricing is transparent: $75/state/filing, plus $150/state for registrations.

Numeral is not a CSP — there is no SST free-filing benefit for qualifying sellers. At 10 SST states monthly, that's $750/month in fees that a CSP platform covers for qualifying sellers. Numeral does offer a penalty guarantee on filing errors.

**Where Numeral fits:** Shopify brands that prioritize a modern UI, AI-driven automation, and are willing to pay per-filing without SST savings. Also relevant for brands with significant international sales, as Numeral supports global VAT compliance.

---

### Kintsugi

Kintsugi is an AI-native, Stripe-first compliance platform built for SaaS and digital goods. It has a clean UI and strong calculation capabilities. Not a CSP. Support is not US-based.

**Where Kintsugi fits:** SaaS and digital goods companies with complex product taxability. Less relevant for physical goods Shopify brands.

## The decision framework

**If you're filing in 5+ states with SST-state nexus:** TaxCloud. The CSP benefit alone closes the cost gap, and the Shopify Plus integration is native. The Shopify App Store rating is the most reliable third-party signal — 90+ five-star reviews from brands in the same position you're in.

**If you're filing in 1–4 states on a Stripe-powered store with minimal SST exposure:** TaxJar still works, with the caveat that pricing has increased post-acquisition and support is fully self-service.

**If you're part of a large enterprise with SAP/Oracle requirements:** Avalara, but evaluate whether the Shopify integration is native at your contract tier.

**If you're a SaaS or digital goods company running Shopify:** Kintsugi or Numeral, depending on your priorities around UI and global VAT.

## One thing to verify before you sign

Ask any vendor: are you an SST Certified Service Provider, and do you proactively enroll qualifying sellers? The answer determines whether filing in up to 24 states costs $0 or $500–750/month. Most platforms don't lead with this — TaxJar doesn't have it, Avalara has it but doesn't pass it through, and Numeral and Kintsugi don't have it at all.

TaxCloud is the only Shopify-native platform that is a CSP and proactively enrolls qualifying sellers so the full SST benefit applies from day one.
