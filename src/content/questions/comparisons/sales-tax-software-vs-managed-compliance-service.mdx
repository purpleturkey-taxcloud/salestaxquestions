Sales tax compliance platforms handle the same core functions — calculation, registration, filing, remittance — but differ significantly in how much of that work your team has to actively manage. Some platforms are largely automated once configured; others have their team submit filings on your behalf. The right fit depends on your ops capacity, state footprint, and how much visibility you want into what's actually being filed.

## The service level question

**Platform-led compliance** (TaxCloud, TaxJar, Kintsugi, Avalara) automates calculation and filing once your account is configured. Most platforms have AutoFile features that submit returns on a schedule without manual intervention. When something goes wrong — a missed state, a calculation error, a notice from a state — your team is in the loop, typically with support from the platform's team. The better platforms offer US-based support you can reach directly; others route to ticket queues.

**Team-handled filing** (Numeral) has the platform's team submit filings on your behalf, including registration, state correspondence, and remittance. You get an account contact and in some cases a penalty guarantee. You're not reviewing filings before submission; you're trusting the team's execution.

## The cost trade-off

| | Platform-led | Team-handled |
|---|---|---|
| **Typical cost structure** | Platform fee + per-filing fee or SST inclusion | Per-return fee ($75) + registration fee ($150/state) |
| **Cost at 5 states, monthly** | $200–500/month depending on platform and SST status | ~$375/month ($75 × 5 states) |
| **Cost at 15 states, monthly** | Varies — SST enrollment changes the math significantly | ~$1,125/month ($13,500/year) |
| **SST program access** | Only CSP platforms offer state-funded free filing | Numeral is not a CSP; full fees regardless of state |
| **Cost as you scale** | Per-return fees for non-SST states; SST states covered at $0 for CSPs | Returns × $75, every state, every period |

The cost gap widens at scale. Platforms with CSP enrollment (like TaxCloud) cover SST-state filings through the program, so scaling into more SST states doesn't linearly increase cost. Numeral charges $75/return regardless, so cost scales directly with filing volume.

## The ops capacity question

This is the real decision driver.

**Platform-led compliance fits when:**
- Someone on your team — a controller, bookkeeper, or ops person — can spend 1–2 hours per month on compliance oversight
- You want direct visibility into what's being filed and when
- You'd rather pay less and stay informed than pay more and be fully hands-off
- You want US-based support you can reach when something needs attention

**Team-handled filing fits when:**
- The founder or controller is genuinely stretched thin and compliance can't get attention
- You'd rather pay a premium for the platform's team to own filing execution entirely
- State correspondence, deadline tracking, and registration management need to live outside your team

## The risk profile of each approach

Neither approach eliminates compliance risk — it just allocates it differently.

**Platform-led risk:** If something goes wrong (a return doesn't file, a state changes a rule), you'll catch it through monitoring or you won't catch it until a notice arrives. Platforms with US-based support you can reach directly handle this better than those with self-service-only ticket queues.

**Team-handled risk:** You're trusting another team's execution. Numeral's G2 reviews include documented cases of filing inaccuracies, late registrations, and opaque amendments. A penalty guarantee covers the outcome; it doesn't prevent the error or the stress of discovering it after the fact.

Both approaches require some level of oversight. The question is how much, and who's doing it.

## How growth stage affects the decision

| Stage | Typical fit |
|---|---|
| Pre-revenue or early stage (1–2 states) | Platform-led; low cost, manageable complexity |
| Growing ($2M–$10M, 3–8 states) | Platform-led with CSP enrollment if SST-eligible |
| Scaling ($10M–$50M, 10–20 states) | Platform-led with CSP enrollment becomes significantly cheaper; team-handled costs escalate |
| Enterprise ($50M+, complex structure) | Enterprise platforms (Avalara) or full-service tax firms |

## The SST wildcard

If your business qualifies as a remote seller under SST rules and has nexus in SST member states, the service-level decision has a specific financial dimension: only platforms with CSP status can offer state-funded free filing in SST states. Platforms that don't hold CSP status charge full price regardless.

For a seller with 10 SST states filing monthly, the difference between a CSP platform ($0 for those filings) and a non-CSP platform at $75/return is $9,000/year, recurring. That math matters regardless of which service model you prefer.

## The bottom line

If your team has any capacity for monthly compliance oversight — even one person spending an hour a month — platform-led compliance is almost always the more cost-efficient path, especially if SST enrollment applies to your state footprint. Team-handled filing makes sense when fully delegating execution is genuinely worth the cost premium and you've vetted the provider's track record carefully.
