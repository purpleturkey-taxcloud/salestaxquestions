If you're looking at this page, you're probably already on Avalara, and something changed. Maybe the price went up. Maybe you can't get anyone on the phone. Maybe your renewal is coming and you're asking whether there's something better for a growing Shopify brand that doesn't have a dedicated tax department.

Here's the honest comparison.

## The thing Avalara won't bring up

Both Avalara and TaxCloud are Certified Service Providers (CSPs) in the Streamlined Sales Tax program. That designation matters because qualifying remote sellers, businesses with no employees or inventory outside their home state, can get registration, calculation, filing, and remittance covered in all 24 SST member states at no cost to them. The state pays the CSP directly.

Avalara has this designation. TaxCloud has this designation. The difference is what each company does with it.

Avalara charges $42–$65 per state per filing period. If your SST state footprint includes 10 states filing monthly, you're paying $420–$650/month ($5,000–$7,800/year) for filings that a properly enrolled CSP could deliver at no additional charge. Avalara's revenue model depends on those fees. They don't advertise that you might not have to pay them.

TaxCloud's model is built around SST enrollment. Qualifying sellers are enrolled and the state-funded coverage applies. Those 10 states cost nothing extra beyond the platform fee.

## What each platform is built for

Avalara is enterprise infrastructure. It was built for large companies with dedicated tax teams, complex ERP requirements, and global operations. When it works, it's powerful. When it doesn't, you're navigating an outsourced support team that customers routinely describe as "can't get ahold of anyone" and "goes overseas."

TaxCloud is built for growing ecommerce brands: the $5M–$50M Shopify-native operator who's a founder, a controller, or a bookkeeper wearing multiple hats. No dedicated tax staff. Just someone who wants this off their plate.

## Where Avalara genuinely wins

If you're running a multi-entity business on NetSuite or SAP, need VAT compliance alongside US sales tax, or have a dedicated tax operations team — Avalara is the right tool. The integration breadth is real. The enterprise capability is real. It's just overkill for a $10M Shopify brand with a lean ops team.

## The honest trade-off with TaxCloud

TaxCloud's dashboard isn't as polished as Avalara's, that's a consistent theme in reviews, and worth knowing upfront. If you need deep custom reporting or complex multi-entity structures, TaxCloud may not be the right fit. It's a platform built for US-focused ecommerce operators who want sales tax off their plate, priced reasonably, with a real person on the other end when something needs attention.

## What the cost difference looks like in practice

The math is straightforward once you know the numbers. Sellers comparing Avalara to TaxCloud on published pricing alone miss the SST enrollment variable, it's the reason cost reductions of 30–50% are commonly reported by businesses that switch.

A mid-market ecommerce brand filing monthly in 10 SST states pays $420–$650/month in Avalara filing fees for those states. Under CSP enrollment at TaxCloud, that same filing is $0. The platform fee difference is real but smaller than the filing fee difference.

TaxCloud's G2 reviews reflect this: reviewers switching from Avalara consistently cite the cost reduction as the primary driver, with US-based support access as the secondary. "An absolute game-changer compared to my previous experience with Avalara" is a representative review, not an outlier.

## Who should stay on Avalara

- You're on NetSuite, SAP, or Oracle and the integration is already embedded
- You have meaningful international revenue that needs VAT/GST alongside US sales tax
- You have a dedicated tax team that can absorb Avalara's support friction

- You're a growing Shopify brand in the $5M–$50M range
