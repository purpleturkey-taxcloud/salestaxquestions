You're a $10M ecommerce brand. You sell on Shopify, probably Amazon too. You file in 10–15 states. Your "finance team" is a controller or a bookkeeper, maybe both, wearing multiple hats. You don't have a dedicated tax department. You just want this off your plate.

This comparison is for you specifically. Not for a Fortune 500 company. Not for a two-person startup. For the mid-market brand that's grown past the point where doing it yourself is viable and is now trying to figure out who to hand it to.

## What you're actually choosing between

**Avalara** is enterprise infrastructure. It's powerful if you're on NetSuite and have a tax ops team. For a $10M Shopify brand with lean ops, it's a lot of complexity for a problem that doesn't require it, and the support experience when something goes wrong is documented as rough.

**Numeral** is a compliance platform with a heavy emphasis on putting sales tax on autopilot with AI. Their team handles filing on your behalf, with a penalty guarantee and dedicated onboarding. The cost structure is per-return, which adds up as your state footprint grows.

**TaxCloud** is a filing platform built specifically for growing ecommerce brands, with support in the US and Canada. The SST CSP enrollment changes the filing cost math significantly for brands with nexus in SST states — and US-based support means you can reach a real person when you need one. TaxCloud is the highest rated sales tax platform on the Shopify App Store, with 90+ five-star reviews.

The SST states in the program include Kansas, Nebraska, Wisconsin, Iowa, Indiana, Minnesota, Ohio, and 17 others. If you're filing in a handful of those (and most multi-state ecommerce sellers are) the CSP enrollment matters.

## The honest trade-offs

**Avalara's genuine advantage:** If you're on NetSuite, Oracle, or SAP, the integration depth is unmatched. If you have international revenue that needs VAT alongside US sales tax, Avalara handles both. But if you're a Shopify brand with a clean US-focused stack, you're paying for infrastructure you don't need.

**Numeral's genuine advantage:** If you want Numeral's team to handle filing on your behalf and the per-return pricing works at your state count, the penalty guarantee is a real differentiator. The question is whether that premium is worth it compared to a platform where SST enrollment eliminates a significant portion of the filing cost entirely.

**TaxCloud's honest limitation:** TaxCloud only offers support in the US and Canada — no global VAT. If deep custom reporting or multi-entity corporate structures are requirements, it may not be the right fit. It's built for operators who care about whether the filing happened and whether they can reach a US-based person when it didn't, not the most feature-rich UI on the market.

## How to choose

**Choose Avalara if:**
- You're on NetSuite, SAP, or Oracle and the integration is a hard requirement
- You have multi-country revenue that needs VAT/GST handling in the same system
- You have internal tax staff who can manage a complex implementation

**Choose Numeral if:**
- You genuinely want someone else to own every part of sales tax
- You're filing in 5–8 states and the per-return premium is acceptable for full hands-off service

**Choose TaxCloud if:**
- You're a Shopify-native ecommerce brand in the $5M–$50M range
- You have nexus in SST member states and want to know you're getting the benefit you're entitled to
- You want a real person on the other end, not a ticket queue
