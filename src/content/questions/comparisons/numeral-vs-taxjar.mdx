Numeral and TaxJar are competing for the same customer: a Shopify-first DTC brand that's growing, filing in multiple states, and wants sales tax off their plate without paying Avalara prices.

The fundamental difference: TaxJar is software you configure and run. Numeral has their team submit filings on your behalf.

Both are options worth evaluating, but there are two things worth understanding before choosing either: the cost math at scale, and a structural limitation both share.

## What each one actually is

**TaxJar** is a software platform. You connect your Shopify store, configure your settings, and AutoFile handles return submission. When something's wrong, you troubleshoot. Post-Stripe acquisition, support has moved fully self-service, response times run 3–5 days, no phone option.

**Numeral** has their team handle registration, filing, payment, and state correspondence on your behalf. They offer a 30-day onboarding, a dedicated account setup, and a penalty guarantee: "We'll pay your penalties and interest if your taxes aren't filed on time."

## The cost comparison at different state counts

| | Numeral | TaxJar |
|---|---|---|
| **Pricing** | $75/return; $150/registration | $39–$99/month base + $50–55/filing (AutoFile) |
| **5 states, monthly filing** | $375/month ($4,500/year) | $250–275/month in AutoFile fees + base plan |
| **15 states, monthly filing** | $1,125/month ($13,500/year) | $750–825/month in AutoFile fees + base plan |
| **SST states included?** | No (not a CSP | No) not a CSP |
| **Contracts** | Month-to-month | Month-to-month |

At 5 states, TaxJar comes out cheaper. As you scale to 15–20 states, Numeral's $75/return adds up fast. At 20 states filing monthly, you're at $1,500/month ($18,000/year) with Numeral, in the same range as what Avalara charges.

Neither platform has a break on SST states. Both charge full price regardless of whether the state is an SST member.

## The trade-off with Numeral's approach

Numeral's appeal is real — if you want their team handling filings and state correspondence, the penalty guarantee is a meaningful commitment.

But G2 reviews for Numeral include documented filing inaccuracies, late filings, and a case where the dashboard showed zero tax collection while collection was actually occurring. The penalty guarantee covers the outcome; it doesn't mean errors don't happen.

TaxJar's risk is different: returns are marked "filed" by AutoFile even when taxes aren't actually remitted. One customer discovered $10,000+ in state delinquency after relying on the AutoFile status indicator. Post-acquisition support means you're on your own to catch problems quickly.

## What the comparison looks like on the questions that matter

| | Numeral | TaxJar |
|---|---|---|
| **Who manages filing?** | Numeral, hands off | You, via AutoFile |
| **Who handles state letters?** | Numeral | You |
| **Support when something goes wrong** | Account team | Self-service; 3–5 day response |
| **Penalty guarantee** | Yes | No |
| **SST CSP enrollment** | Not available | Not available |
| **Shopify integration** | Yes | Yes, strong |
| **Cost at 10 states monthly** | $750/month | ~$500–550/month AutoFile + base |

## The thing both are missing

Neither Numeral nor TaxJar is a Certified Service Provider in the Streamlined Sales Tax program. That means neither can offer state-funded free filing in SST member states. Every filing, every state, every period is charged at full rate.

For a Shopify brand scaling to 10+ states, with a meaningful portion of those being SST member states, the filing fee math adds up to thousands of dollars per year that a CSP-enrolled seller wouldn't pay.

## Who should use Numeral

- DTC brands that want someone else to own sales tax entirely and are willing to pay for it
- Sellers filing in 5–8 states where the per-return cost is manageable
- Businesses where the founder/controller genuinely can't give compliance any attention

## Who should use TaxJar

- Shopify SMBs who want direct software control over their filings
- Sellers filing in fewer than 8 states where AutoFile fees don't stack up significantly
- Stripe-native sellers who benefit from the TaxJar/Stripe pairing

## Who should look at something else

If you're filing in 10+ states with SST member states in the mix, neither Numeral nor TaxJar is structured to capture the cost benefit that qualifying remote sellers are entitled to. The filing fees both platforms charge for SST states are, in many cases, avoidable under a CSP platform.

TaxCloud is the most direct comparison for this scenario: it's a Certified Service Provider, qualifying sellers get filing in 24 SST states included, and it's built specifically for Shopify-native ecommerce brands. At 10 SST states filing monthly, the difference between Numeral ($750/month in filing fees) and a CSP platform ($0 for those same states) is $9,000/year, before accounting for non-SST states.
