Above $100K: California, Texas ($500K each), New York ($500K AND 100 transactions), Alabama and Mississippi ($250K). No states go below $100K. For most sellers, the $100K standard applies everywhere except these five states.

## Key takeaways

- **$500,000 threshold:** California, Texas, New York, sellers below this level have no economic nexus in these states regardless of transaction count (with NY's AND caveat)
- **New York's AND condition:** NY requires $500,000 in sales AND 100+ transactions, both must be met. A seller with $600K in NY sales but only 80 transactions does not have NY economic nexus
- **$250,000 threshold:** Alabama and Mississippi — 2.5× the standard; many mid-market sellers clear $100K in these states but not $250K
- No US state uses a threshold below $100,000
- The higher thresholds in large states mean sellers can have significant sales volume in CA, TX, or NY without triggering economic nexus, physical nexus (FBA, employees, warehouse) still applies regardless of threshold
- When planning nexus exposure, treat CA, TX, NY, AL, and MS as separate threshold buckets from the standard $100K states
