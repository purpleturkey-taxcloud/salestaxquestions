No. Physical nexus rules are fully intact and in many cases create obligations before economic nexus would kick in. The two are additive, either alone triggers registration. Physical presence has no threshold at all; economic nexus has a dollar threshold.

## Key takeaways

- Physical nexus and economic nexus are independent, either alone requires registration; having both just confirms the same obligation
- Physical presence (FBA inventory, warehouse, employees, sales reps) creates nexus immediately with no minimum sales threshold
- Economic nexus requires crossing a dollar or transaction threshold, which takes time to accumulate
- FBA sellers commonly discover physical nexus in 10–15 states where their Amazon inventory is stored, well before they'd cross economic nexus thresholds there
- The Wayfair decision expanded the universe of states where remote sellers owe tax, it didn't narrow physical nexus rules
- States audit both types of nexus independently; a clean economic nexus analysis doesn't protect against undisclosed physical nexus exposure
